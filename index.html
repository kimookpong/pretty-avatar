pretty-avatar — avatars that look back

A cute animated avatar for the top of your page. It turns its head to follow the reader’s cursor and reacts when they poke it.

  • 68 ready-made characters — animals and people
  • Zero-dependency React component, about 10 kB, SSR-safe
  • An AI agent skill that draws your own character
